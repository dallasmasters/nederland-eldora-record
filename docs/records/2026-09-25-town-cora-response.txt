TOWN'S RESPONSE (Sept. 25, 2026)

From: Macy Caligaris, CMC, Town Clerk, Town of Nederland <townclerk@nederlandco.org>
Subject: RE: Online Form Submittal: Public Records Request Form

The Town of Nederland is in receipt of your request below.

The Town conducted a reasonable search for records responsive to your request. Under the Colorado Open Records
Act ("CORA"), the Town's obligation is to identify and provide existing public records that are responsive to a
request. CORA does not require the Town to create records that do not already exist, prepare new records, or
answer questions. Rather, CORA requires the disclosure of existing public records maintained by the Town.

In response to your request, the Town is providing the responsive records it identified, including account
statements and a spreadsheet compiled and maintained by the Town. Additionally, the Town's 2025 audit report is
publicly available and may be accessed at the following link:
https://nederlandco.org/DocumentCenter/View/594/2025-Nederland-Financials-Audit.

The Town believes the records provided are responsive to your request and represent the public records identified
through its reasonable search. We recognize that the materials provided may not fully answer every question
reflected in your request; however, the Town's obligation under CORA is to provide existing records. Accordingly,
the Town has responded to your request in accordance with CORA, C.R.S. § 24-72-201 et seq.
