Regarding 3, there are no existing records responsive to this request.
However, I am going to provide a narrative explanation to help clarify how
the Town manages and accounts for its funds. It is important to note that,
under CORA, the Town is not obligated to create new records or provide
explanations beyond the production of existing responsive records.
Nevertheless, I believe providing this additional context will be helpful
in understanding the Town's financial structure.
The Town does not maintain separate bank account records for each
individual fund because it operates under a pooled cash structure. Under
this structure, the Town's cash is held in five accounts: two CSAFE
accounts and three UMB accounts (two money market accounts and one checking
account). Rather than maintaining separate bank accounts for each fund, the
Town utilizes its general ledger to track and allocate cash balances to the
appropriate funds.
2 and 3 took 20 minutes, so I used the remaining 40 minutes to pull some of
the contracts and service agreements you're seeking (ones in red below are
the ones that are attached).
