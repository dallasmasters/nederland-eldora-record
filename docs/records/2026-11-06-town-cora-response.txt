TOWN'S RESPONSE (Nov. 6, 2026)

From: Miranda Gordon, Interim Town Manager & Zoning Administrator, Town of Nederland <townadmin@nederlandco.org>
Subject: Re: Online Form Submittal: Public Records Request Form

The Town of Nederland is a party to a confidentiality agreement with POWDR concerning POWDR's confidential
commercial and trade secret information. The Plante Moran report contains information that POWDR has identified
as confidential and subject to that agreement and to the protections for trade secrets and confidential
commercial information under C.R.S. § 24-72-204(3)(a)(IV).

POWDR has taken the position that the report is confidential in its entirety. The Town has asked POWDR to
reconsider that position because portions of the report appear not to contain confidential information. The
Town is continuing to consult with POWDR regarding what portions of the report may be disclosed and will respond
to your CORA request once that review is complete.

THE REQUEST (Oct. 23, 2026), as submitted; requester's details omitted

Under the Colorado Open Records Act, I request a copy of the following existing public record. Electronic copies
by email are fine.

The report Plante & Moran, PLLC prepared for the Town under its Aug. 12, 2025, engagement letter for financial
due diligence on Eldora Mountain Resort ("PM Nederland Eldora QoE Engagement Letter"), described in that letter
as "an executive summary memorandum." If there is more than one version, the most recent final version is enough.

I understand that the seller's confidential financial data in the report may be withheld. I am asking for the
parts that are not exempt: the report's date, title, and scope, and any conclusions, summary findings, or
limitations that don't disclose the seller's confidential financial data, with the rest redacted. If any part is
withheld, please give me the specific grounds in writing (C.R.S. 24-72-204(4)). If the search will take more than
the free first hour, please send me a cost estimate before you start.
